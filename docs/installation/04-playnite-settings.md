<div class="xn-tutorial__notes" tabindex="0" markdown="1">
<div data-guide-note-start="0" data-guide-note-end="0" markdown="1">

In Playnite Desktop, open **Menu > Settings (F4)**. Follow the recommended X-Nite settings in the next screenshots.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="1" data-guide-note-end="1" markdown="1">

Under **Appearance > Grid View**, set **Target aspect ratio: 1:1** and **Stretch mode: Uniform to fill**. Covers become square and fill the cards. These options also affect Fullscreen mode.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="2" data-guide-note-end="2" markdown="1">

Under **Advanced > Performance**, choose **Image rendering scaler: Quality** and save. This is the recommended visual setting; consult the performance guide if needed.

</div>
