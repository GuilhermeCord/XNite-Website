<div data-guide-note-start="0" data-guide-note-end="0" markdown="1">

Read the terms of use, mark the acceptance checkbox if you agree, then choose **Create profile**. After creation, select your new profile to enter Home.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="1" data-guide-note-end="1" markdown="1">

### Updating your achievements

After creating and selecting your first profile, X-Nite starts preparing your achievements in the background. Open your profile to follow the indicator at the top right of **Welcome**, **Social** and **Gaming**. The list may be empty while data loads.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="2" data-guide-note-end="2" markdown="1">

### Your achievements are available

As data becomes available, games appear under **Gaming**. Select a game to view its achievements. The games and numbers in this screenshot are examples from the account used.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="3" data-guide-note-end="3" markdown="1">

### Updating friends’ achievements

After your achievement stage, preparation continues with friends. Open a **friend’s full profile** to follow the indicator at the top right. This notice appears on the friend’s profile, not your own.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="4" data-guide-note-end="4" markdown="1">

### Friend achievements are available

Friend games and achievements appear as Playnite Achievements makes data available. Availability depends on the connected account, privacy and platform responses. You can keep using X-Nite during collection. The indicator appears during initial preparation and manual updates requested in **Settings**; a full update is not required every time you open Playnite. The count represents processed items, not necessarily successful requests.

</div>
