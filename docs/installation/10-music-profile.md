<div data-guide-note-start="1" data-guide-note-end="1" markdown="1">

<span class="xn-guide-optional">Optional</span>

Open `Menu > UniPlaySong Settings > Quick Start`. Choose a preset for the music behaviour you want. The screenshot shows personal radio/Spotify preferences; they are **not required** by X-Nite. For music associated with a selected game, review the corresponding preset instead. Skip this UniPlaySong setup if you do not want menu music.

[Skip music setup →](14-download-xnite.md)

!!! success "Before continuing"
    A preset is selected, or you chose to skip this optional integration.
</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="2" data-guide-note-end="2" markdown="1">

<span class="xn-guide-optional">Optional</span>

In UniPlaySong, open **Playback** and review your chosen preset. Change only the settings you need; radio mode is not necessary for game music. Playback depends on a configured source or tracks available for your games.

!!! success "Before continuing"
    Playback matches the preset you chose.
</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="3" data-guide-note-end="3" markdown="1">

<span class="xn-guide-optional">Optional</span>

In `Playback > Default Music`, select your source. A local music pool needs accessible audio files; a Spotify-based mode needs Spotify running and configured in UniPlaySong. The paths in the screenshot belong to the example installation. Use your own files and account.

!!! success "Before continuing"
    The chosen source can play a track before you switch to Fullscreen.
</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="4" data-guide-note-end="4" markdown="1">

<span class="xn-guide-optional">Optional</span>

Open `UniPlaySong > Pauses > Common Events`. Enable **Pause on game launch** if menu music should stop when a game starts. Review the separate radio exception if you deliberately want radio to continue. Lock, idle and window-focus pauses are personal preferences. Save and test a game launch.

!!! success "Before continuing"
    Music pauses or continues during gameplay as intended.
</div>
