# Choose a music profile

<span class="xn-guide-optional">Optional</span>

Open `Menu > UniPlaySong Settings > Quick Start`. Choose a preset for the music behaviour you want. The screenshot shows personal radio/Spotify preferences; they are **not required** by X-Nite. For music associated with a selected game, review the corresponding preset instead. Skip the four UniPlaySong steps if you do not want menu music.
