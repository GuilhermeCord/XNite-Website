<div class="xn-tutorial__notes" tabindex="0" markdown="1">
<div data-guide-note-start="0" data-guide-note-end="0" markdown="1">

In Desktop mode, open **Menu > Add-ons (F9)**.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="1" data-guide-note-end="1" markdown="1">

Open **Extension settings > Generic > Playnite Achievements**.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="2" data-guide-note-end="2" markdown="1">

Under **General > Sync & Updates**, use the recommended settings shown: your refresh every **2 hours**, friends’ **Recent** refresh every **12 hours**, and monitoring while a game runs. Playnite Achievements manages these intervals.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="3" data-guide-note-end="3" markdown="1">

Under **Platforms > Steam**, select **Enable this platform** and **Sign in**. Use the account connected in the Steam client so friends and achievements match. Configure other platforms only if you use them.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="4" data-guide-note-end="4" markdown="1">

Choose **Check** and confirm **Authenticated**. Signing in grants account access; it does not finish the initial synchronization.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="5" data-guide-note-end="5" markdown="1">

Under **Friends**, select **Steam** and **Include unowned games in refresh** to include friends’ games you do not own. Use **Refresh Friends List**, save and complete the initial synchronization in Playnite Achievements. This can take time; Hub online presence is read separately from the Steam client.

</div>
