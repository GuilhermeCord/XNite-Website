<div class="xn-tutorial__notes" tabindex="0" markdown="1">
Open `Add-ons > Extension settings > Generic > Playnite Achievements`. Review **Sync & Updates**, then open `Platforms > Steam`. Enable Steam, choose **Sign in**, authenticate and select **Check**. Wait for the authenticated state shown below, then complete a sync. This account supplies your personal achievements and Gamescore. The Steam client supplies the live friend list independently.

!!! success "Before continuing"
    Steam is authenticated and the first achievement sync has finished.
