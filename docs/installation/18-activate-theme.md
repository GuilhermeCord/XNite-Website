<div class="xn-tutorial__notes" tabindex="0" markdown="1">
<div data-guide-note-start="0" data-guide-note-end="0" markdown="1">

In Desktop mode, use **Menu > Switch to Fullscreen Mode** or press **F11**.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="1" data-guide-note-end="1" markdown="1">

In Playnite’s default Fullscreen interface, open the menu and select **Settings**.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="2" data-guide-note-end="2" markdown="1">

Open **Visuals**, where you choose the theme used by Fullscreen mode.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="3" data-guide-note-end="3" markdown="1">

Under **Theme**, select **X-Nite — Xbox Experience for Playnite**.

</div>

<div data-guide-note-start="4" data-guide-note-end="4" markdown="1">

Confirm the restart with **Yes** to apply the theme. After it reopens, continue to choose the initial language and create your profile.

</div>
