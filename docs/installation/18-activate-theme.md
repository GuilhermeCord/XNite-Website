# Activate X-Nite and finish

Press **F11** to enter Fullscreen. Open `Menu > Settings > Visuals`, select **X-Nite — Xbox Experience for Playnite** under **Theme** and confirm the restart. Menu names vary with Playnite’s language. Complete [First setup](../first-setup.md) and create your profile. X-Nite initially uses English; choose your profile language during setup.
