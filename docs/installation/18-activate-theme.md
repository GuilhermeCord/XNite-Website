<div class="xn-tutorial__notes" tabindex="0" markdown="1">
Press **F11** to enter Fullscreen. Open `Menu > Settings > Visuals`, select **X-Nite — Xbox Experience for Playnite** under **Theme** and confirm the restart. Menu names vary with Playnite’s language. Complete [First launch](19-first-launch.md) and create your profile. X-Nite initially uses English; choose your profile language during setup.

!!! success "Before continuing"
    X-Nite is selected and Fullscreen opens. Continue to stage 4 to create your profile.
